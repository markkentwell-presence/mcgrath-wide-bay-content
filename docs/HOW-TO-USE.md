# McGrath Wide Bay content plugin: how to use it

A quick guide for the team. Five minutes to set up, then about ten minutes a post.

## 1. Set it up once

- **Claude (recommended):** Customize > Plugins > Add > Upload plugin, choose `mcgrath-wide-bay-content-plugin.zip`. If plugins are not on your plan, upload the six skill zips under Customize > Skills.
- **ChatGPT or Copilot:** create a Custom GPT or Copilot agent. Paste `CORE-INSTRUCTIONS.md` into Instructions and attach `MCGRATH-WIDE-BAY-CONTENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` as knowledge.
- **Edit your office details once:** open `OFFICE-PROFILE.md`, check the office list (address, phone and email for Bundaberg, Bargara, Hervey Bay, Maryborough, Gladstone and Agnes Water), delete any office that does not use the kit, change the words after each colon (licensee, sign-off), save it and upload it to your Claude Project, Custom GPT or Copilot agent. To change something later, edit and re-upload, or just ask: "Update our office profile: ...".
- Each presenter fills in a profile once (name, role, office, suburbs, years local, camera comfort). The assistant will offer to save it.

## 2. Every Monday: plan the week

Type: **"Plan our four posts for this week for Bundaberg."** (or Bargara, Hervey Bay, Maryborough, Gladstone, Agnes Water). Paste what is new: listings, rentals, sold results, local events.

You get four posts across the five goals, with no more than one pure listing post.

1. A sellers or landlords carousel.
2. A buyer walk-and-talk video.
3. A community or personal brand post.
4. A buyers or tenants post.

## 3. Make each post

Type: **"Make post 2"**, or start fresh with **"I want a post."** The assistant asks four questions.

1. Who is presenting?
2. What is it for? Buyers, tenants, landlords, sellers, or brand.
3. Which property or topic, and which office or suburb? Paste the listing link.
4. Video to camera or carousel?

**Pick the office for every post.** The assistant tells you which office it used (for example "Using the Hervey Bay office: 07 4121 0616"). If that is wrong, say so and it swaps the phone, email and sign-off.

**Video:** you get a teleprompter script, a shot list and captions for each platform.

- Film on a phone in 9:16 with a mic.
- Use Teleprompter Pro as a safety net. Do not read it word for word.
- Then ask: "Give me the edit steps for CapCut."

**Carousel:** you get slide-by-slide copy, a caption and step-by-step Canva instructions in McGrath charcoal, orange and off-white.

## 4. Check before you post

Type: **"Check this."** and paste the caption or script. You get PASS or FIX on:

- price and auction rules
- rental rules
- agency name (a licence number is not required on social posts in Queensland; turn the licensee line on in your office profile if head office wants it)
- consent
- hype and AI-sounding phrases.

If you have Humanizer, run the caption through it, then check again.

## 5. Rules of thumb

- Never open on features. Open on the viewer: "Here's what $X buys you in {suburb}", "Don't put that number on your house", "Three things buyers miss at open homes here".
- Every line needs a "so what" for the person watching. Sell the life and the decision, not the specs.
- The assistant scores every script out of 10 and only hands it back at 8.5 or better.
- Give value first. Make one soft ask at the end.
- No TikTok post says "link in bio" or mentions Instagram.
- Never invent features, distances or stats. Confirm anything marked [confirm].
- Get the vendor's OK before filming a home, and consent from anyone on camera.
- Check McGrath head office brand and compliance guidelines and your licensee-in-charge for anything borderline.

The assistant drafts. A person approves and posts.
